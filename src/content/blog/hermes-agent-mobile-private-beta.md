Hermes Agent now has a mobile app, and it is in Private Beta. A small group can try it before the wider release.

The team says the app lets you work from anywhere. You still get the customization and power that Hermes is known for, now on your phone.

A short video tour shows the app's features, options, and capabilities. If you want to see the app before you can try it yourself, the tour is a good place to start.
