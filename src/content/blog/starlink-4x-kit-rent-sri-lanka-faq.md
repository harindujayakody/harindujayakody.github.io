Starlink's Standard (4X) Kit rent in Sri Lanka raised a lot of questions, so I put the common ones into a support ticket. Starlink Support answered them over a phone call, and everything below comes from that call.

## Can I rent a Starlink Mini?

No. Rent is only available for the Standard Kit, and you have to order it on the Starlink website.

## How long do I have to activate the service after the Kit arrives?

You have 7 days from the day the Kit reaches you. If you haven't activated it by day 7, the monthly bill starts on its own.

## Do I have to use it for 12 months? What if I don't want it?

No. There is no minimum period before you can cancel, so you can use it for a month or two and stop.

Once you cancel, you have 30 days from the day the service ends to return the Kit. If it doesn't go back in time, Starlink charges you the Kit's full value.

If you decide you don't want it before activating, you can return the Kit undamaged within 7 days of receiving it.

## How do I cancel and send the Kit back?

1. Log in to your starlink.com account and cancel the service.
2. Starlink emails you the date the service ends, how to send the Kit back, and a return label.
3. Give the Kit to the carrier within 30 days of the end date in that email.

## Do I own the Kit after 12 months?

No. Twelve months of rent doesn't give you the Standard Kit, and the rent you paid isn't taken off its price.

## How do I buy the Kit, and what does it cost?

To own the Kit you buy it out at its full value (the replacement cost). Your account shows the amount and asks you to confirm before any money is taken.

Starlink hasn't published a fixed buyout price, so the amount can't be quoted in advance.

## Will I get an ownership letter after 12 months?

No, since ownership doesn't transfer on its own. You become the legal owner once you pay the full amount and complete the buyout.

## Can I move to Residential Lite later?

No. A Kit from the rental scheme can't be used on Residential Lite. For that plan you have to buy out the Kit first.

## Quick summary

- Activate within 7 days of delivery.
- No 12-month minimum. You can cancel whenever you like.
- After cancelling, return the Kit within 30 days. If you don't, the Kit's value is added to your account.
- The Kit isn't yours after 12 months. Ownership needs a buyout.
- Residential Lite needs a buyout first.

## Calling Starlink Support

When you open the ticket, write "Phone call is most preferred" and Starlink will call you. My call was with USA Customer Service.
